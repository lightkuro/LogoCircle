Last updated 24 November 2026

LogoCircle is a tool for drawing logos that runs in your web browser. Once the page has loaded it works almost entirely on your own device, and it can keep working offline. There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for. This page explains what that means for your information, and the terms on which the tool is offered. By using LogoCircle you agree to them.

Privacy

What stays on your device. Everything you make or bring in stays in your browser's own storage on your device: your logos, the outlines and colors in them, photos you add, fonts you load, and your settings. None of it is sent to us, and we cannot see it.

What reaches a server. Loading the site means your browser asks our host for the app's files (its code, styles, icons and the bundled fonts). Like any website, the host receives the technical details that come with such requests, such as your IP address, browser type and the time. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles these requests and may keep short-term logs to run and protect its network, under its own privacy policy. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party scripts.

Your browser's storage. LogoCircle uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to keep your work and preferences, and a service worker to keep a copy of the app for offline use. These stay on your device. You can remove them at any time by deleting your logos in the app or by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings. Browsers can also clear this storage themselves, for example when space runs low or in private browsing.

The clipboard and sharing. LogoCircle reads your clipboard only when you choose to paste, and your browser may ask for your permission first. Exported files are saved or shared through your own device, to wherever you choose.

Children. LogoCircle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anyone's personal information.
